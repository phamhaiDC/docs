Booking status lifecycle

PendingCreated automatically when the customer submits a booking on the website.
→
ConfirmedSet by customer service after calling the customer; the system sends a ZNS/SMS.
→
CompletedUpdated automatically when the customer pays on the POS.

Deposit branch: customer service sends a QR code, and the system checks the transaction automatically and records Deposit paid.

Customer

  1. 1
    Books on the website: chooses the restaurant, date, time and number of guests, and fills in contact details.
    → Booking status is Pending
  2. 3b
    If a deposit is requested: scans the QR code received and transfers the deposit.
  3. 6
    Finishes the meal and pays at the restaurant.
    → The system sets Completed

Customer service

  1. 2
    Receives the new booking and calls the customer to confirm.
  2. 3
    Changes the booking status to Confirmed.
    → The system sends a ZNS/SMS to the customer automatically
  3. 3b
    When a deposit is needed: creates a QR code on the booking and sends it to the customer.
    → The system checks the transaction and confirms the deposit automatically

Restaurant

  1. 4
    Customer arrives: staff find the booking by booking code or phone number.
  2. 5
    Open the booking, select "Assign table" and pick a table on the POS.
    → The POS opens an order for that table automatically
  3. ↳
    Serve the meal, then take payment on the POS (step 6).
